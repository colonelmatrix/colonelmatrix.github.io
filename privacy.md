# Privacy policy — Barad-dur Photos

Last updated: 12 September 2026

## Purpose and access

Barad-dur Photos is a personal photo-management setup operated by colonelmatrix. It uses rclone to access the owner’s Google Drive and Immich to organise photos and review duplicates.

Google Drive access is authorised by the owner through Google’s sign-in and consent process. The setup accesses photo and video files, folder information and related metadata. Authorised operations include reading, copying, uploading, organising and deleting files.

## Storage and use

Original media is stored in the owner’s Google Drive. Files may also be cached on the owner’s server during processing. Immich stores its database, generated previews and other processing data on the owner’s infrastructure.

Data is used for personal photo management. It is not sold, used for advertising or made publicly available through this website.

Google processes data as the storage and authentication provider. This information website is hosted by GitHub Pages; photo files and Google access credentials are not published in its repository.

## Retention and control

Files and processing data are retained until the owner removes them, subject to any configured trash, cache and backup retention.

The owner can revoke the application’s access through their Google Account’s third-party connections settings. Revoking access stops future authorised access but does not automatically delete previously stored files, caches or backups.

## Google API data

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

## Contact

For questions about this application, contact the operator through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/colonelmatrix). Do not include private photos, credentials or other sensitive information in public issues.
